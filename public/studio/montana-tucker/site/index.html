In Full

From every angle.

Enduring artists don't only entertain. They empower. Montana does both, from every angle.

She began modeling in commercials at eight, then danced for Ashanti and Ashlee Simpson. Her first release, "Ain't No Stressin'," landed on the Step Up 2: The Streets soundtrack, and "Candy Swirl" followed in Bring It On. She teamed with Flo Rida on "Turn It Up," opened for Ciara and Lil Wayne, acted in the Emmy-winning series The Bay, and hosted the Streamy Purpose Awards. Her videos reach millions, and her debut single "Hola" arrived on Mr. 305 Records.
